The Note-Taking Decision

This project aims to persuade high school and college students that taking math notes is much better on pen and paper than electronically. Because the target audience is very familiar with digital tools, the "modality" for this argument is an interactive website that mimics the choices students face every day.

NOTE: the ASCII arts might not display properly on smaller devices, but the text and the experience should still work as intended.
